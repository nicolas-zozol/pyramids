// what we believe

principles, written down.

  • we read before we write.
    no commits in the first week. promise.
  • boring tech, on purpose.
    postgres, sql, http. fewer moving parts is the feature.
  • the next engineer matters most.
    every choice is a memo to whoever inherits it.
  • we don't take work we can't finish.
    small slate. long horizons. quarter-shaped projects.
  • tests are documentation.
    if it isn't covered, we don't know how it should behave.
  • incidents are not surprises.
    they are the signal that an invariant was wrong.
