# Epic : Pyramid v2

**Dernière mise à jour :** 2026-08-02
**Epic :** pyramid-v2-epic
**Infix :** PYRAMID

## Epic

As the publisher of the Robusta sites, I want the Pyramids base to turn content plus a design system into a deployable SEO site, so that the next site ships without being rewritten from scratch.

Pyramid v1 failed at exactly that: the base and the site grew into each other, and neither could be reused. Version 2 restarts the base and proves it on a single site, robusta.build, before anything else moves onto it.

## Contexte & architecture cible

The shared base is a Vercel + Next.js App Router backbone with React Server Components and ISR, plus `pyramids-helpers` and two packages of its own: `@robusta/pyramids-routing` since 2026-07-31 and `@robusta/pyramids-content` since 2026-08-01. The three other shared packages — `pyramids-layouts`, `pyramids-links`, `pyramids-ctas` — render DaisyUI classes and are deprecated by the shadcn decision of 2026-07-30; the v2 site imports none of them. What a site brings of its own is its content, its design system and its `seopyramids.config.ts`. Full picture in `root.archi.md`, vocabulary in `ubiquitous-language.md`.

Three shifts define v2:

- A new site workspace, `apps/robusta-build`, live since 2026-07-31. `apps/robusta` is not refactored — it is a v1 that gets retired, not repaired.
- A design system shipped as a package, one per site. `@robusta/pyramids-design-system` is robusta's; another site carries its own (BR-PYRAMID-3).
- URLs that state what they address, so a page resolves without reading the content source (BR-PYRAMID-1). Delivered by seo-url-scheme: the discriminants `l`, `c`, `p` and a reserved `t`, under a content root the site names — `articles` for robusta.build. The shapes are in that story and in `apps/robusta-build/README.md`; no sub-categories.

The corpus the v2 site serves is the 11 articles of `apps/robusta/content/blog`, the ones the v1 code actually read. Since 2026-08-01 they live under `apps/robusta-build/content/articles`, copied rather than moved, converted to the v2 schema — flat categories, mandatory `published: true`, translation identifiers on the two translated pairs — with the 44 images they reference published under an asset root the site names for itself, outside the content section; the v1 tree is frozen the day the copy was taken. Markdown in the repository is the content source of v2, read at build time and never on a served request (BR-PYRAMID-7). Telemetry stays available in the base but switched off for the v2 site, and visitor intents are not tracked (BR-PYRAMID-2).

Since 2026-07-31 the repository names its own toolchain — yarn 4.17.1, Node 22, `nodeLinker: node-modules` — and the green set builds end to end from a clean checkout (BR-PYRAMID-5). That is the baseline `apps/robusta-build` was created on.

SEO excellence runs across the whole epic rather than sitting in one deliverable: metadata, sitemap, structured data, internal linking — deliberately not aggressive.

## Business Rules

- BR-PYRAMID-1 — The URL of a page must state the kind of page it addresses, so that a site can resolve it without consulting its content source.
- BR-PYRAMID-2 — A site built on the version 2 base must not track visitor intents.
- BR-PYRAMID-3 — Each site must carry its own design system, which no other site may reuse.
- BR-PYRAMID-5 — The build chain of a site must complete from a clean checkout of the repository.
- BR-PYRAMID-6 — A site's design tokens must come from its design system alone.
- BR-PYRAMID-7 — A site must not read its content source while serving a request.
- BR-PYRAMID-8 — A site must supply the page copy of every page it publishes; its design system must supply no page copy.
- BR-PYRAMID-9 — An article carries at most one category and any number of tags.
- BR-PYRAMID-10 — A site publishes an article only if that article declares itself published.
