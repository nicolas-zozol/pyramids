# Epic : Pyramid v2

**Dernière mise à jour :** 2026-08-01
**Epic :** pyramid-v2-epic
**Infix :** PYRAMID

## Epic

As the publisher of the Robusta sites, I want the Pyramids base to turn content plus a design system into a deployable SEO site, so that the next site ships without being rewritten from scratch.

Pyramid v1 failed at exactly that: the base and the site grew into each other, and neither could be reused. Version 2 restarts the base and proves it on a single site, robusta.build, before anything else moves onto it.

## Contexte & architecture cible

The shared base is a Vercel + Next.js App Router backbone with React Server Components and ISR, plus `pyramids-helpers` and, since 2026-07-31, `@robusta/pyramids-routing` and `@robusta/pyramids-content`. The three other shared packages — `pyramids-layouts`, `pyramids-links`, `pyramids-ctas` — render DaisyUI classes and are deprecated by the shadcn decision of 2026-07-30; the v2 site imports none of them. What a site brings of its own is its content, its design system and its `seopyramids.config.ts`. Full picture in `root.archi.md`, vocabulary in `ubiquitous-language.md`.

Three shifts define v2:

- A new site workspace, `apps/robusta-build`, live since 2026-07-31. `apps/robusta` is not refactored — it is a v1 that gets retired, not repaired.
- A design system shipped as a package, one per site. `@robusta/pyramids-design-system` is robusta's; another site carries its own (BR-PYRAMID-3).
- URLs that state what they address, so a page resolves without reading the content source (BR-PYRAMID-1). Delivered by seo-url-scheme: the discriminants `l`, `c`, `p` and a reserved `t`, under a content root the site names — `articles` for robusta.build. The shapes are in that story and in `apps/robusta-build/README.md`; no sub-categories.

The corpus the v2 site must serve is the 11 articles of `apps/robusta/content/blog`, the ones the v1 code actually reads; their images sit under `content/blog/**/images` and travel with them. Since 2026-08-01 the articles are copied rather than moved, the v1 tree frozen the day the copy is taken, and the images they reference are published under an asset root the site names for itself, outside the content section. Telemetry stays available in the base but switched off for the v2 site, and visitor intents are not tracked (BR-PYRAMID-2).

Since 2026-07-31 the repository names its own toolchain — yarn 4.17.1, Node 22, `nodeLinker: node-modules` — and the green set builds end to end from a clean checkout (BR-PYRAMID-5). That is the baseline `apps/robusta-build` was created on.

SEO excellence runs across the whole epic rather than sitting in one deliverable: metadata, sitemap, structured data, internal linking — deliberately not aggressive.

## Business Rules

- BR-PYRAMID-1 — The URL of a page must state the kind of page it addresses, so that a site can resolve it without consulting its content source.
- BR-PYRAMID-2 — A site built on the version 2 base must not track visitor intents.
- BR-PYRAMID-3 — Each site must carry its own design system, which no other site may reuse.
- BR-PYRAMID-5 — The build chain of a site must complete from a clean checkout of the repository.
- BR-PYRAMID-6 — A site's design tokens must come from its design system alone.
- BR-PYRAMID-7 — A site must not read its content source while serving a request.
- BR-PYRAMID-8 — A site must supply the page copy of every page it publishes; its design system must supply no page copy.
- BR-PYRAMID-9 — An article carries at most one category and any number of tags.
- BR-PYRAMID-10 — A site publishes an article only if that article declares itself published.

Verified entry by entry against `business-rules.md` on 2026-07-31, three times: the two lists match. The first two passes recorded and modified nothing; the third recorded BR-PYRAMID-10 and is traced below. BR-PYRAMID-4 is retired and never reused: the identifier carried two different meanings in two days. Two rules raised by the epic's stories are validated by the human but not recorded — "an indexable page must carry content of its own", which the registered glossary definition now restates, and "a site must keep serving an article at every address under which it has published it", still waiting on a glossary term for a published address. Both are traced in the decisions below. BR-PYRAMID-9 was recorded by `bulkman resolve` as delegated registrar; the vocabulary gap BR-PYRAMID-5 carried is closed by the glossary entries for clean checkout and green set. BR-PYRAMID-10 was recorded by epicman as registrar, once `Published article` existed in `ubiquitous-language.md`.

Verified again on 2026-08-01 and nothing recorded: the nine entries above match the registry, and the one term arbitrated that day — `Asset root` — is a definition, which the jurisdiction test routes to `ubiquitous-language.md` and never to `business-rules.md`.
